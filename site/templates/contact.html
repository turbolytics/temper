For vendors

For companies that sell a database, a streaming system or another data product.

Compatibility report

Many products sell on a claim such as "Postgres-compatible" or "Kafka-compatible". We test the claim against the real system, feature by feature, and show what matches, what doesn't, and what you document as different. You get a matrix page with your branding, hosted independently and dated with the versions tested, a PDF for enterprise sales and security reviews, and a badge that links to the live matrix. We rerun it on every release of your product and of the system it is compatible with. Every report carries the line "Independently tested by Temper by Turbolytics", and failures stay visible.

Full audit

We list every operation your users rely on and test each one three ways: against your documentation, against a reference system such as PostgreSQL or Apache Kafka, and under real failures such as crashes, network cuts and slow storage. You get a matrix of what holds and what doesn't, and a one-command reproduction for every bug.

Conformance kit

A conformance kit is a test suite you hand to everyone who writes a client, connector or backend for your product. Each one proves it behaves the same way before it ships. The kit keeps working after our engagement ends.

Docs accuracy check

We find the places where your documentation and your product's behavior disagree. Every audit we have run has found some. It's a small, fixed-price job.

Release gate

Before a major version or general availability, we rerun your guarantees against the new build and check that data written by the old version still reads on the new one.

For teams that run or buy software

Vendor evaluation

Before you commit to a database or streaming vendor, we test the candidates against their own claims, on workloads shaped like yours. You decide with evidence, not datasheets.

Incident follow-up

After an outage, we turn the postmortem into written guarantees and regression tests, so the same failure can't return unnoticed.

Test suite audit

We find the tests that are skipped, expected to fail, or assert nothing, and show which promises your suite doesn't actually check.

AI workflow setup

We set up your team's coding agents so every feature comes with declared guarantees, and the build fails when a guarantee has no proof.

For investors and acquirers

Technical due diligence

Before a funding round or an acquisition, we check whether the product does what its pitch deck says.
